Crop disease detection

Early crop disease
detection from a phone photo.

Detect plant diseases in seconds, so a grower can act while the problem is still small. A leaf disease caught a week early is a crop saved, and a phone is the only sensor that scales to every row of every field.

Point it at an apple or grape leaf. It names the disease, reports its confidence in that answer, and paints the exact pixels it used to decide - so you can tell a real diagnosis from a lucky guess.

Who it serves: growers and agronomists scouting rows with a phone, greenhouse and vertical-farm teams checking crops daily, and agritech products that need a model small enough to run on the device, with no cloud GPU.
